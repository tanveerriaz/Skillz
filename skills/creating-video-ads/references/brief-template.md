## Story Table

| Time | Purpose | Voiceover | Visible proof | On-screen text | Asset | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–15% | Hook | One concrete problem | Local/product context | Short headline | Strongest truthful visual | Branded transition |
| 15–35% | Positioning | What the product actually is | Real product surface | Category/format clarity | Browser/app capture | Branded transition |
| 35–55% | Action | What the user does | Real interaction | Plain-language action | Screen recording | Branded transition |
| 55–80% | Proof | Verified value proposition | Real result/data | Source or benefit | Result capture | Branded transition |
| 80–100% | CTA | Exact conversion action | URL/store/contact proof | CTA and disclaimer | Brand end card | Hold 2.5s+ |

## Asset Priority

1. Real product captures and project-owned assets.
2. Licensed, context-specific footage or photography.
3. Generated visuals for missing illustrative shots, never fake testimonials or fabricated product evidence.

## Audio Map

Generate one narration file per scene. Record target start/end, generated duration, pronunciation notes, rate adjustment, and final duration. Rewrite first; use small tempo changes second.

## Acceptance

Copy the applicable checks from [quality-gates.md](quality-gates.md) into the production plan. Define objective pass evidence for every check.
