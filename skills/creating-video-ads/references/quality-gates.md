# Video Ad Quality Gates

## Product and Claims

- Inspect the current product, not only the supplied brief.
- Match product category and CTA: browser, native app, ordering, discovery, SaaS, marketplace, or service.
- Verify every count, rating, price, partner, endorsement, certification, trust, and availability claim.
- Show real result names/data when the ad references them.
- Include affiliation, data-source, expiry, licensing, or regulated-product disclaimers when required.

## Security and Rights

- Scan source/artifact folders for API keys, bearer tokens, passwords, private `.env` data, and personal information without printing values.
- Do not commit, push, publish, deploy, upload, or spend without authorization.
- Record asset ownership or licensing. Obtain releases when identifiable people or venues require them.

## Visual and Cultural Quality

- Use real product captures; never fabricate evidence screens.
- Make location, community, cuisine, or industry specificity recognizable and accurate.
- Avoid stereotypes and generic global stock for hyperlocal products.
- Keep text inside safe areas, readable on a phone, and visible long enough.
- Inspect hero frames and transitions, not only source HTML.
- Ensure motion supports comprehension; every scene needs an intentional entrance and transition.

## Voice and Music

- Use a natural voice appropriate to audience and brand.
- Correct names, acronyms, URLs, and local-language pronunciation.
- Generate scene-aligned takes. Rewrite or adjust speaking rate before mechanical compression; avoid large tempo shifts.
- Keep narration intelligible above music and sound effects.
- Verify captions against final spoken audio.

## Render

- Lint, validate, inspect, and snapshot when the framework supports them.
- Review all errors and warnings; document why any remaining warning is non-blocking.
- Confirm duration, dimensions, frame rate, video/audio codecs, and full-file decode.
- Spot-check rendered frames across the timeline for frozen footage, black frames, clipping, and incorrect CTA.

## Delivery

- Use a distinct filename so earlier versions remain comparable.
- Copy only to a user-authorized destination.
- Verify source and delivered files with `cmp` and SHA-256.
- Report the exact file path, technical metadata, verification evidence, and any unresolved limitation.
