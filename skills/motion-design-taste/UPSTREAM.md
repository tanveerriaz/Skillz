# motion-design-taste: motion-design-taste

Source: original Skillz skill — no upstream repository. Written from the taste rules learned across the November 2026 video production runs: reference reels watched in full, blunt verdicts on successive cuts of the same film, and the fixes each verdict produced.

Licence: MIT.

This skill records design judgement, not a tool. Its rules are portable principles — one idea at a time, huge negative space, one accent per scene, real imagery framed, odometer counters, kinetic type that leads the beat, music that matches the animation's energy — each learned from a specific reference or a specific failed cut. No person's name, site, project, or private detail is in it; the references are described by technique, and the verdicts are quoted only as examples of plain judgement.

## What changed

Nothing upstream to diff against. The rules were written fresh in the Skillz nine-section format from the production record: the matte-black-squircle minimalism, the maximalist kinetic-typography recipe, the rolling-counter feel, the cut-paper look-check win, and the voice/music mismatch that forced a music swap on an otherwise approved cut.

## Install and prerequisites

None. Use it as a judging layer alongside any video build skill.

## Caveats

- Taste rules are defaults, not laws: a maximalist cut and a minimalist cut are judged by different games, and the skill says so. Name the game before judging.
- The rules were learned from short-form narrated video (30–60 s, 9:16). They transfer to other short-form work; longform has its own pacing and is out of scope.
