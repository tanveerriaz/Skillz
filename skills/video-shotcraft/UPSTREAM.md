## What changed

`SKILL.md` was written fresh in the Skillz nine-section format from the upstream skill's documented workflow: read-only product inspection → real-page capture → product brief → storyboard from shot recipe cards → Remotion project → beat sync and sound design → frame QA → MP4 plus Motion Workbench. No upstream files, scripts, or assets are bundled here.

## Install and prerequisites

Hand the agent the link ("Install this skill for me: https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft") or `git clone https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft.git`. Needs Remotion and a headless browser for capture.

## Caveats

- The shot library is described as 124 recipe cards / 150 shots in 10 categories in the upstream README; counts move as the repo updates.
- 149 bundled SFX assets are listed in the upstream repo. Check their licences before a commercial launch; Freesound downloads carry per-sound licences.
- Remotion licensing: free for individuals/small teams, paid licence may apply for companies.
